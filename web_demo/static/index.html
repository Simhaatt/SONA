    About SONA

    The models inside the app and the benchmark results behind them.

    • Learned models beat classical filters by a wide margin. On VoiceBank every classical method has a negative SI-SDR improvement; every learned model gains about 9 to 10 dB.
    • Unfamiliar audio is the hard part. On MS-SNSD, a corpus never seen in training, GTCRN's gain falls from 9.9 dB to 3.2 dB, and intelligibility stays at the original level (STOI 0.818 before, 0.816 after).
    • Adapting on the fly did not pay off. Unguarded test-time adaptation hurts quality; with the strict guard it is safe but the gain is about zero.
    • The complex mask is a trade-off. +0.31 dB SI-SDR and slightly higher intelligibility, but 0.016 lower PESQ.
    • Cleaner for people is not cleaner for machines. Whisper's word error rate rose about 24 % on enhanced audio.
